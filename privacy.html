Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Feelascope publishes animated parenting videos and a free guide and newsletter for parents. This policy explains what data we use and why.

The Feelascope Uploader app

The "Feelascope Uploader" app is an internal tool used only by Feelascope to upload its own videos to its own YouTube channel and social media accounts.

What data the app uses

The app uses Google's YouTube Data API, the TikTok Content Posting API and the Meta Graph API to upload videos, titles, captions and thumbnails to Feelascope's own YouTube channel, TikTok account, Instagram account and Facebook page. It only reads the basic profile information needed to post (account ID, display name, posting settings). It only accesses the Feelascope accounts that authorised it. It does not access, collect, store or share data of any other user.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens are stored encrypted and used only to publish Feelascope's own content. No personal data is sold or shared with third parties.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google) or in the TikTok app under Settings → Security → Manage app permissions.

Feelascope's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Uploads use the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Newsletter and free guide

When you sign up for our free guide or newsletter, we collect your email address and, if you choose to give it, your first name. We use them only to send you the guide and our weekly email, and to read your replies. We never sell or share your data.

Our email service is Kit (ConvertKit LLC), which stores and sends the emails on our behalf as a data processor. Kit is based in the United States; transfers are protected by standard contractual clauses and Kit's data processing agreement. You confirm your signup by email (double opt-in).

We keep your data until you unsubscribe. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link. You can also email feelascope@gmail.com to see, correct or delete your data. If you live in the EU, you have the right to complain to your data protection authority (in the Netherlands: the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses no tracking or advertising cookies and no analytics. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses for security; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.
